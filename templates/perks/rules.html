Rules

Register to join the Partner Perks program. (You’ve already done this…YAY!)

Every time you sell an ACDI Solution, login to your Partner Perks account and submit your deal on the claim form.

Your ACDI Partner Perks Reloadable VISA card is mailed to you.

A percentage of ACDI’s suggested retail price on all approved products and services from claimed and paid ACDI invoices will be loaded onto your Partner Perks VISA card!

At ACDI, we believe that solid partnerships help build mutual success. We want to reward you for your hard work. Up to two Partner Perks members are eligible for the reward.

Only orders that have been processed through ACDI's Order Processing department are eligible to be claimed. For new participants - You may claim rewards for sales that occur back to the first of the month in which you enroll. Once enrolled, deals must be claimed within 90 days of the sale date.
